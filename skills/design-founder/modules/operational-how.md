# Operational how

**Purpose.** Turn an accepted idea into something that can actually run on day one: cold start, supply, who does what, and what stays manual.

**Enters when.** Any big idea is accepted (a product, a marketplace, a content or community feature, anything with supply or moderation).

**Stays quiet when.** Execution details are already settled.

**Exits when.** Each question below has an owner and a day-one answer, even if the answer is "manual, by the founder".

## Questions to answer (not all to ask; answer what you can, ask up to 3)

1. **Cold start.** What does the first user see on day one? If the value depends on other users or content, where does the first batch come from?
2. **Supply.** Who creates the content, inventory, or listings? Why would they? What's the smallest supply that makes the product worth opening?
3. **Day-one operator.** Who runs it: curates, moderates, answers support, fixes data? How many hours a week does that take?
4. **Manual first.** Which parts can be done by hand for the first 100 users (curation, matching, onboarding calls, payouts)? Automate only what hurts.
5. **Moderation.** What gets reviewed before it's visible, and what after? What's the response time?
6. **Failure handling.** When a provider, payment, or data source fails, what does the user see and who gets alerted?
7. **The loophole.** Is there an existing behavior, community, or platform to borrow supply from instead of building it? (People already love giving advice: capture that instead of asking them to write reviews.)

## Output shape

```
Day one
- First screen shows: <what, from where>
- Supply: <source>, seeded by <who>, <how many> before launch
- Operator: <who>, <hours/week>, doing <tasks>
- Manual until <threshold>: <list>
- Moderation: <pre/post>, <who>, <response time>
- Borrowed supply: <loophole, if any>
```

## Writes to the brain

- `decisions.md`: day-one operating model.
- `backlog.md`: automation items with the threshold that triggers them.
- `state.md`: operational risks parked to launch.

## Example

Local places app, cold start.
First screen: 50 places near the user, all founder-curated in two neighborhoods; outside those, an honest "we're not here yet, suggest a place" state.
Supply: founder visits and verifies 10 places a week; friends in a WhatsApp group suggest places, founder verifies before they go live.
Loophole: people already send each other Instagram reels of places; a share-to-app action turns a saved reel into a planned visit.
Manual until 500 active users: verification, moderation of suggestions.
