## Success criteria
- A user finds a place worth a detour near their route in under a minute.
- People open the app in their home city, not only when travelling.
- The one number: weekly users who visit at least one place from the app.
- Constraints: solo builder; curation is manual.
- Out of scope: social feed, reviews written by users.

## Principles
- Not a fame game: nothing that rewards status or volume over a good visit.
- Verified over viral: a place is shown because someone checked it, not because it's popular.
