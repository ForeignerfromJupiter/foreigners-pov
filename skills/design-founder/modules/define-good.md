# Define good

**Purpose.** Agree what success means before designing, so every later choice can be scored against it.

**Enters when.** Before designing any medium or large problem, and success isn't already in `state.md`.

**Exits when.** The person confirms the criteria. This is checkpoint 2.

## Produce

1. **2 to 4 success criteria.** Each is observable: a behavior, not a feeling. "A first-time visitor can place an order in under a minute", not "easy to use".
2. **The one number that proves value.** The single metric that would move if the product works, and whether it's instrumented today. If it isn't, that's the first build task.
   A number that decides whether to build for a channel can't be measured only inside that channel (smartphone share measured on the website only counts smartphone users). Measure across every channel, or ask people directly.
3. **Constraints.** Time, money, team, platform, legal, brand. Only the ones that change choices.
4. **2 or 3 principles specific to this problem.** A principle must rule something out. "Not a fame game" rules out public leaderboards; "simple and delightful" rules out nothing, so it's not a principle.
5. **Out of scope.** What we are deliberately not solving now.

## Questions (max 3, with likely answers)

- "The one number: <guess>, measured <how>. Right?"
- "Principle guess: <X>, which rules out <Y>. Keep it?"
- "Hard constraint I should know: <guess, e.g. two weeks, one engineer>?"

## Writes to the brain

- `state.md`: Success criteria, The one number, Constraints, Out of scope, Principles.
- `decisions.md`: the criteria as a decision, dated.

## Example

Bakery landing page.
- Criteria: a visitor knows what's baked today within 5 seconds; can order for pickup in 3 taps on a phone; finds hours and address without scrolling on mobile.
- One number: pickup orders per week from the site (needs an order button with tracking).
- Constraints: owner updates it themselves from a phone; no budget for photography beyond their own.
- Principles: "Today's bake first" (rules out a hero about the founding story); "Readable in a queue" (rules out small text over photos).
- Out of scope: delivery, loyalty program, blog.
